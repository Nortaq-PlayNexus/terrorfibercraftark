# FAQ — Terror Fibercraft 1000x

**Q: Is this Steam or Ark Ascended?**
A: No. It's ARK: Survival Evolved (ASE) on the Microsoft Store / Xbox CrossPlay
build only — Xbox One/Series + Windows 10/11. No Steam, no ASA.

**Q: Can I play on my Xbox with a friend on Windows?**
A: Yes, that's the whole point — true CrossPlay via Nitrado's clustered build.

**Q: Why Nitrado? Can't you self-host?**
A: The Microsoft Store version can't be natively clustered or headless-hosted.
Nitrado runs the only CrossPlay cluster build. See `00_RESEARCH/research-ms-store-xbox-constraints.md`.

**Q: What are the rates?**
A: 1000x harvest, 1000x taming, 1000x XP, near-instant breeding (EggHatch/BabyMature
1000, cuddle matched). Full tuned sheet: `03_CLUSTER_DESIGN/server-configs/rates-1000x.md`.

**Q: Which maps?**
A: 8 at full scale — Island, Scorched Earth, Aberration, Extinction, Ragnarok,
Valguero, Crystal Isles, Fjordur. All are **free ASE maps**. Alpha launches with 3, then weekly rollout.

**Q: Is it PvP or PvE?**
A: The public cluster is PvP. Private rented clusters are PvE by default (PvP add-on available).

**Q: Do you sell items/levels for money?**
A: Never. That violates the ARK EULA and Xbox ToS. We only take donations and
offer private-server hosting. Perks are cosmetic/community only. (`legal-compliance.md`)

**Q: What are the "custom caves" / sculpted bases?**
A: Admins use floating terminal structures to sculpt desirable raid/defense spots
(caves, sealed ruins, plateau/island bases). It's admin terrain only — players
boxing paths with walls is a bannable exploit. (`custom-caves-manifest.md`)

**Q: Will my base get wiped?**
A: No full wipes on the public cluster. Only performance wipes with 7-day notice.
See `06_OPERATIONS/wipe-policy.md`.

**Q: How do I become a Founder?**
A: Join Discord and pick a Founder tier on Patreon/Tip4Serv. (`07_WEBSITE/patreon-tiers.md`)

## 2026 quick-answer set

**Q: Is this Ark Ascended (ASA)?**
A: **No.** Terror Fibercraft 1000x is **ARK: Survival Evolved (ASE)** only — the
Microsoft Store / Xbox CrossPlay build. We do not run ASA. (Source:
`00_RESEARCH/research-2026-update.md` §1.)

**Q: Can Steam players join?**
A: **No.** CrossPlay is **Xbox ↔ Microsoft Store PC only**. Steam (and ASA) players
cannot join. This is a hard platform limit of the MS Store ASE build.

**Q: What about Astraeos / Genesis?**
A: **Astraeos is ASA-only** (ARK: Survival Ascended paid DLC) — it does **not** exist
on ASE, so it is **not** on our cluster; please don't expect it. **Genesis Part 1 & 2**
are **paid ASE DLC** and are offered only as optional *private* add-on maps, not on the
free public cluster. The 8 free ASE maps we run are listed under "Which maps?" above.
See `00_RESEARCH/research-2026-update.md` §2.

**Q: Are donations pay-to-win?**
A: No. Donations/tips fund hosting only. Perks are roles, colors, and community
access — never items, levels, or advantage. ARK Code of Conduct compliant.
(`07_WEBSITE/donation-store.md`, `01_BUSINESS/legal-compliance.md`)
